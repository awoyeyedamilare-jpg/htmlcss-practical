Virgo ♍ Zodiac Sign

Virgo is the sixth sign of the zodiac. It is associated with people born between August 23 and September 22. Virgo is an Earth sign traditionally ruled by Mercury.

Virgo Personality Attributes

Virgos are often described as practical, organized, thoughtful, hardworking, analytical, and detail-oriented. They usually value honesty, responsibility, and helping others.

Virgo Strengths

  • Reliable and responsible
  • Careful and observant
  • Practical problem-solvers
  • Helpful and dedicated

Virgo Challenges

  • May overthink situations
  • Can be overly critical or perfectionistic
  • May worry about small details
